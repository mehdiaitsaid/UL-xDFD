# How to cite UL-xDFD

Please cite the **version** of the specification or repository snapshot that you used, and identify which specific research components were involved (for example, the annotated graph model, AI metering profile, or synthetic example).

Suggested temporary reference until the research team assigns formal authorship, a publication and a repository URL:

> UL-xDFD research contributors. (2026). *UL-xDFD: Unified Lifecycle Extended Data Flow Diagram, research specification v1.0*. Unpublished research proposal / repository documentation. \[Repository URL and persistent archive to be added by maintainers\](https://github.com/mehdiaitsaid/UL-xDFD).

**Before public release:** replace the collective placeholder with approved authors or organizational attribution, add the canonical GitHub address and a persistent identifier (for example a Zenodo DOI if archived), and check citation-policy requirements. Do not claim peer review, an official standard, or empirical validation without evidence.

The separate [sources page](docs/standards-and-references.md) credits the external standards and projects whose terminology informed this proposal.

# Appendix B. Recommended research and stakeholder instruments




## B.1 Stakeholder workshop sequence

- Product/finance: define value unit, success definition, business activity and cost inclusion/exclusion.
- Architect/developer: enumerate systems, processes, stores, external providers, contracts and unowned dependencies.
- Platform/QA/DevSecOps: reconstruct delivery workflows, tests, approvals, security gates and runner costs.
- SRE/operations: inspect deployment, observability, incidents, recovery and capacity constraints.
- Finance/FinOps/procurement: reconcile usage to ledger, identify commitments, variable and fixed costs.
- HR/management/team representatives: collect skills and culture evidence ethically with sample limits.
- Independent analysts: review annotation disputes, blind overlap, unknown/expiry and chosen scenarios.
- Research lead: freeze the baseline, define intervention, preregister later outcomes and assign follow-up sources.

## B.2 Example interview questions

- Which user journeys produce value, and how is one successful outcome counted?
- What data enters/leaves each process, and are request and response contracts documented?
- Which requirements are mandatory and how do we prove they are met?
- Who can approve a release, security exception, major spend or production rollback?
- Which build/test/scan tasks run for every change, and which are conditional?
- Which dependencies cause slow handoffs, manual approvals or production incidents?
- Which parts of shared infrastructure and shared staff costs are attributed today?
- Which facts are telemetry-backed, self-reported, estimated or unknown, and who can verify them?
- Which organizational constructs require voluntary survey evidence rather than structural inference?
- What would count as a failed adoption and how would the outcome be observed months later?

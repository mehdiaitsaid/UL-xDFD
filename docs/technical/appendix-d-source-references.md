Research status and disclosure. UL-xDFD v0.1 is a new conceptual research proposal that merges user-provided specifications and adds a lifecycle-oriented semantic contract and reference implementation assets. It is not ISO/OMG/FinOps standardization, and quantitative formulas are analytical starting points requiring measurement and local calibration. The example is synthetic. A comprehensive prior-art review, formal semantics verification, industrial pilots, and prospective validation are necessary before generalizing claims about cost forecasting, optimization efficacy or adoption success.
